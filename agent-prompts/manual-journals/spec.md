Manual journals (G/L journal entries, "Asientos Manuales"): double-entry journals posted to the ledger. This spec was named `simple-g-l-journal` until ETP-5720; that name no longer exists. Entity `gLJournal` is the journal header, `gLJournalLine` its lines. To record a journal: (1) etendo_create on `gLJournal` (ledger, currency, document type and date are defaulted; etendo_schema with view:"create" is the authority on what a create needs); (2) etendo_create on `gLJournalLine` once per line, with parentId = the journal id, an `accountingCombination` that is a posting account of the journal's ledger (summary accounts are rejected) and either `foreignCurrencyDebit` or `foreignCurrencyCredit`; (3) etendo_action `documentAction` with parameters {docAction:"CO"} completes it, which requires total debit = total credit > 0; (4) etendo_action `post` posts it to the ledger. A completed journal that is not posted can be reactivated with docAction "RE"; a posted one must be unposted first (etendo_action `unpost`).
