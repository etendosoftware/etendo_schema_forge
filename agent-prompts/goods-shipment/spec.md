Goods shipments (sales side): goods shipped to a customer. Entity `goodsShipment` is the header, `goodsShipmentLine` the lines. Two ways to put sales-order lines on a shipment. FAST PATH, a whole order in one step: etendo_action `createShipment` on the completed sales order (spec sales-order, entity header) creates a DRAFT shipment with every pending line. FILLING A DRAFT SHIPMENT, which also lets one shipment take lines from several orders of the same customer, in this order: (1) create the header with etendo_create on `goodsShipment` (businessPartner and the rest are defaulted; etendo_schema with view:"create" is the authority on what a create needs); (2) etendo_action `availableOrders` on the shipment id lists the customer's completed sales orders, in the shipment's currency, that still have quantity to ship; (3) `availableOrderLines` with {orderId} lists that order's pending lines, each with its pendingQuantity; (4) `importOrderLines` on the shipment id with {orderId} imports everything still pending, or with {orderId, lines:[{sourceLineId, quantity}]} exactly those lines (repeat for each order); (5) confirm the shipment with the DocAction button (action documentAction, docAction CO). The shipment must be a draft and the order completed, of the same customer and currency. What is pending already subtracts what any draft shipment holds, this one included: asking for more answers 422 importQuantityExceedsPending (naming the product, the requested and the pending quantity) and adds nothing, and the order's delivered quantity only moves when the shipment is completed. The shipment is linked to the order when every line it holds comes from that one order. The classic Create Lines From button is redirected to importOrderLines. To invoice a shipment use createDraftInvoice; to take goods back from the customer use createReturn. etendo_schema with view:"actions" returns each action's parameters.
