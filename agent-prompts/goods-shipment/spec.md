Goods shipments (sales side): goods shipped to a customer. Entity `goodsShipment` is the header, `goodsShipmentLine` the lines. Two ways to put sales-order lines on a shipment. FAST PATH, a whole order in one step: etendo_action `createShipment` on the completed sales order (spec sales-order, entity header) creates a DRAFT shipment with every pending line. FILLING A DRAFT SHIPMENT, which also lets one shipment take lines from several orders of the same customer, in this order: (1) create the header with etendo_create on `goodsShipment` (businessPartner and the rest are defaulted; etendo_schema with view:"create" is the authority on what a create needs); (2) etendo_action `availableOrders` on the shipment id lists the customer's completed sales orders, in the shipment's currency, that still have quantity to ship; (3) `availableOrderLines` with {orderId} lists that order's pending lines, each with its pendingQuantity; (4) `importOrderLines` on the shipment id with {orderId} imports everything still pending, or with {orderId, lines:[{sourceLineId, quantity}]} exactly those lines (repeat for each order); (5) confirm the shipment with the DocAction button (action documentAction, docAction CO). The shipment must be a draft and the order completed, of the same customer and currency. What is pending already subtracts what any draft shipment holds, this one included: asking for more answers 422 importQuantityExceedsPending (naming the product, the requested and the pending quantity) and adds nothing, and the order's delivered quantity only moves when the shipment is completed. The shipment is linked to the order when every line it holds comes from that one order. The classic Create Lines From button is redirected to importOrderLines. To invoice a shipment use createDraftInvoice; to take goods back from the customer use createReturn. Reading a shipment: documentAction on the record is the next default action of the document (what its Process button would run); "--" means none, the document is in a final state. invoiceStatus is the percentage of the shipment already invoiced (0 nothing, 100 all, a partial in between); linkedOrders, linkedInvoices and returnReceipts list the documents tied to the shipment (each with id, documentNo and documentStatus) and canCreateReturn says whether something is still returnable. They are returned on a single-record etendo_get (invoiceStatus on lists too). What can be done NOW: before offering an action on a shipment call etendo_schema with view:"actions" and id = the shipment id; each action carries availableNow (true or false) and, when false, unavailableReason, and documentAction carries availableValuesNow. Unavailable actions stay listed so you can explain why to the user. This is advice, not a lock: the server still validates every call. A completed shipment cannot be reactivated or voided through MCP (documentAction offers CO only, to complete a draft): correct it with createReturn. Posting a just-completed document (action post) can answer 409 costNotCalculated with retryAfterSeconds 30: Etendo calculates cost in the background about every 30 seconds, nothing was posted, so wait and repeat the same post. If it answers costCalculationStalled instead (retryable false, no retryAfterSeconds), costing is stuck, not slow: do not retry, tell the user an administrator must check the Costing Background process and the transactions waiting for cost. Book and unbook the document with the post and unpost actions (they appear in the actions view with availableNow); the AD Posted button is hidden and must not be used. post applies to a completed document that is not posted, unpost to a completed one that is. etendo_schema with view:"actions" returns each action's parameters.
