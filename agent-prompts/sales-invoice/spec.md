Sales invoices: customer invoices. Entity `header` is the invoice, `lines` its lines. Two ways to put order or shipment lines on an invoice. FAST PATH, one document in one step: etendo_action `createDraftInvoice` on a completed sales order (spec sales-order) or goods shipment (spec goods-shipment) creates AND completes an invoice with its pending lines. FILLING A DRAFT INVOICE, which also lets one invoice take lines from several documents of the same customer and currency, in this order: (1) create the header with etendo_create on `header` (businessPartner and the rest are defaulted; etendo_schema with view:"create" is the authority on what a create needs); (2) from sales orders: etendo_action `availableOrders` on the invoice id lists the customer's completed orders that still have quantity to invoice, `availableOrderLines` with {orderId} lists their pending lines, and `importOrderLines` with {orderId} (everything pending) or {orderId, lines:[{sourceLineId, quantity}]} (exactly those) adds them; from goods shipments: `availableShipments`, `availableShipmentLines` with {shipmentId} and `importShipmentLines` with {shipmentId, lines?} do the same; repeat for each document; (3) complete the invoice with the DocAction button (action documentAction, docAction CO) — importing never completes it. The invoice must be a draft and the source completed, of the same customer and currency. What is pending subtracts what completed invoices AND draft invoices hold, this one included: asking for more answers 422 importQuantityExceedsPending (naming the product, the requested and the pending quantity) and adds nothing. Price, tax and discount are copied from the order line; the order's invoiced quantity only moves when the invoice is completed. From an order, the quantity of a line goes first to its completed shipments not yet invoiced (oldest first, each up to its own pending, linked to the shipment so it shows as invoiced) and the rest, ordered and not delivered or already taken by another draft, is invoiced against the order line alone, so an invoice can be filled from the order or from the shipment (importShipmentLines) alike, whatever the shipment was already invoiced in a draft, and the two never take the same quantity (the pending of an order line counts every draft invoice). The invoice's order reference is set when every line comes from one order and left empty when several are mixed. The classic Create From buttons are redirected to the import actions, which are for a NORMAL invoice. A RECTIFICATIVE invoice (a draft whose document type is Factura Rectificativa) takes no lines from orders or shipments: importOrderLines and importShipmentLines answer 422 importNotAvailableOnRectificative and create nothing, and the two imports below answer 422 importRequiresRectificative on a normal invoice. On a draft rectificative, in this order: (1) from a customer return: etendo_action `availableReturns` on the invoice id lists the customer's completed returns that still have quantity to credit, `availableReturnLines` with {returnId} lists their lines, and `importReturnLines` with {returnId} (everything pending) or {returnId, lines:[{sourceLineId, quantity}]} (exactly those; quantity is always a positive number) adds them as NEGATIVE lines linked to the return line, priced at the CURRENT price list, not at the price of the invoice they came from (for that, createReturnInvoice on the return); a quantity already held by any invoice line linked to that return line, whatever the status of its invoice, is not offered again; (2) from the invoice it corrects: `availableOriginInvoices`, `availableOriginInvoiceLines` with {invoiceId} and `importOriginInvoiceLines` with {invoiceId, lines?} add NEGATIVE lines, partial quantities allowed up to the source line's quantity, with the product, price, tax and discount of the source line, and link the source invoice in the Rectificaciones tab (the return import does not link); a source line already imported into this invoice is not offered again, and a rectificative is never a valid source. Same customer and currency, the invoice must be a draft, importing never completes it, and the total discount is not carried. Reading an invoice: documentAction on the record is the next default action of the document (what its Process button would run); "--" means none, the document is in a final state. What can be done NOW: before offering an action on an invoice call etendo_schema with view:"actions" and id = the invoice id; each action carries availableNow (true or false) and, when false, unavailableReason, and documentAction carries availableValuesNow. Unavailable actions stay listed so you can explain why to the user. This is advice, not a lock: the server still validates every call. documentAction follows the document: a draft takes CO, a completed invoice takes only RE (reactivate, ruled out when the invoice was accepted by a fiscal system and is already rectified; on a posted invoice RE first removes its accounting entries (un-post) and then reactivates it, and the answer lists steps; an issued invoice is corrected by reactivating it or with a factura rectificativa made in the app); only CO and RE are accepted, any other documentAction value is refused with 422 listing the allowed ones; availableValuesNow lists them for the record. post needs a completed invoice that is not posted yet and unpost the opposite. Posting a just-completed document (action post) can answer 409 costNotCalculated with retryAfterSeconds 30: Etendo calculates cost in the background about every 30 seconds, nothing was posted, so wait and repeat the same post. If it answers costCalculationStalled instead (retryable false, no retryAfterSeconds), costing is stuck, not slow: do not retry, tell the user an administrator must check the Costing Background process and the transactions waiting for cost. etendo_schema with view:"actions" returns each action's parameters. Document tools. Several invoices at once (up to 50, one result each, not atomic): etendo_action_batch (e.g. complete them all; reactivating a posted invoice un-posts it first). Attachments: etendo_request_attachment_upload, etendo_list_attachments, etendo_delete_attachment; do not pass main:true here, the main file is the document's own PDF. PDF: etendo_get_document_pdf returns the stored PDF; a draft has none, and on no_pdf or pdf_stale the user opens the document's preview in the application. Email: etendo_send_document_email (preview:true first).
