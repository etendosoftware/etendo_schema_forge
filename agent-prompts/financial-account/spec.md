Company financial accounts (Etendo table FIN_Financial_Account): the bank, cash and card accounts owned by the company itself, together with their transactions, imported bank statements and reconciliations. The `account` entity represents the company's OWN accounts (required fields: name, currency, type). These are NOT a contact's bank details (a contact is the adopted term for what Etendo classic calls a business partner / BP). To store a contact's bank account (C_BP_BankAccount) use the `contacts` spec (bankAccount entity) instead.
