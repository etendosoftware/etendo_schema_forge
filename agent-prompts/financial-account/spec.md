Company financial accounts (Etendo table FIN_Financial_Account): the bank, cash and card accounts owned by the company itself, together with their transactions, imported bank statements and reconciliations. The `account` entity represents the company's OWN accounts (required fields: name, currency, type). These are NOT a contact's bank details (a contact is the adopted term for what Etendo classic calls a business partner / BP). To store a contact's bank account (C_BP_BankAccount) use the `contacts` spec (bankAccount entity) instead. To reconcile bank statement lines, never press the account's Core buttons: use the `bank-reconciliation` spec through neo_action (entity `bank-reconciliation`, id = the financial account id). neo_schema(spec:"bank-reconciliation", entity:"bank-reconciliation", view:"actions") lists its actions (pendingLines, candidates, autoMatch, reconcileGroup, reconcileDifference, applySuggestions, undoReconciliation, removeOperation, reactivateSelected) and their parameters. Never create or edit bank statements by writing the importedBankStatements / bankStatementLines entities (a statement written there is never processed and skips the UI's checks): create, import, process and reactivate them through the `bank-statements` spec with neo_action (entity `bank-statements`). neo_schema(spec:"bank-statements", entity:"bank-statements", view:"actions") lists its actions (listStatements, statementLines, createStatement, importStatement, processStatement, reactivateStatement), their parameters and what id means for each (the financial account id for listStatements/createStatement/importStatement, the statement id for the others). A statement's lines can only be reconciled once it is processed.
