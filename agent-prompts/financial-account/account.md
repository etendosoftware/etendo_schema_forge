The `account` entity represents the company's OWN financial accounts (Etendo table FIN_Financial_Account): its bank, cash and card accounts (required fields: name, currency, type, country — country is mandatory for every account type and is never derived from the IBAN; on update, a Bank account that has an IBAN must keep a country), including their IBAN, SWIFT/BIC and account number. These are NOT a contact's bank details — for a contact's (business partner / BP) bank account (C_BP_BankAccount) use the `contacts` spec (`bankAccount` entity) instead.

Bank statements: create, preview or import them with the `bank-statements` spec actions — `etendo_action {spec:"bank-statements", entity:"bank-statements", id:<this financial account id>, action:"createStatement"|"previewStatement"|"importStatement"}`; the catalogue is `etendo_schema({spec:"bank-statements", view:"actions"})`. Read statements with etendo_list/etendo_get on `importedBankStatements` / `bankStatementLines`; generic writes on those entities are refused with 405.

Manual movements of the account (a deposit or a withdrawal booked against a G/L item, what the Movements tab records): use this entity's actions — `etendo_action {spec:"financial-account", entity:"account", id:<this financial account id>, action:"createMovement"|"updateMovement"|"processMovement"|"reactivateMovement"|"deleteMovement"}`, with `listMovements` and `movementGlItems` to read; the catalogue is `etendo_schema({spec:"financial-account", entity:"account", view:"actions"})`. A movement is not a bank-statement line: a statement line is what the bank reports, waiting to be matched in a reconciliation. Transfers between two of the company's accounts: `transferFunds` (id = the source account; `transferDestinations` lists the destinations and today's rate between currencies). A transfer is dated today and cannot be deleted; undo it with a transfer back.
