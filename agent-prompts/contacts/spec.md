Master data for contacts (the adopted term for what Etendo classic calls a business partner, also referred to as BP): customers, vendors and employees, with their contact people, locations, discounts and bank accounts. The `bankAccount` entity holds a contact's (business partner / BP) OWN bank accounts (Etendo table C_BP_BankAccount), used to store the contact's payment/bank details. These are NOT the company's own financial accounts. For the company's cash/bank/card accounts (FIN_Financial_Account) use the `financial-account` spec instead.

The contact itself is the `businessPartner` entity: its name, tax ID, email (`etgoEmail`), phone and website are fields of that record, set on create. The `contact` entity is a different thing: the people who work for the contact, each with their own name and email.
